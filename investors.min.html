Investment Process

1

Initial Consultation

Connect with our investment advisor to understand your portfolio requirements, risk profile, and investment goals. We discuss both project-level and equity options available.

2

Project Presentation

Review detailed project documentation, financial models, and market analysis. Site visits and project team interactions can be arranged based on your preference.

3

Due Diligence

We arrange independent audits and verification through our legal partners. All documentation is thoroughly reviewed to ensure regulatory compliance and investment security.

4

Agreement & Funding

Execute investment agreement with clear terms, schedules, and return projections. Funds are deposited into escrow accounts managed by our legal partners for security.

5

Monitoring & Reports

Receive regular (monthly/quarterly) progress updates, financial statements, and project milestones. Investor dashboard provides real-time visibility into all metrics.

6

Exit & Returns

Upon project completion, returns are distributed as per agreement. Multiple exit options available including refinancing, secondary market, or direct acquisition.
